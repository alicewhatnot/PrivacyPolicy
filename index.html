01

Information We Collect

The information collected by an application depends on its features and how you use it.

Some applications may store information that you create within the app, such as documents, notes, settings, or other user-generated content. Where applicable, this information may be stored locally on your device or using services provided by Apple, such as iCloud and CloudKit.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise for a particular application, we do not operate servers or databases that store the content you create within our applications, and we do not have access to content stored privately on your device or in your personal iCloud account.

02

iCloud and Cloud Services

Some applications may use Apple's iCloud or CloudKit services to store and synchronise data between your devices.

Where an application uses these services, the information stored through them is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's terms and privacy policies.

We do not have access to information stored in a user's private iCloud container.

Not all applications use iCloud or other cloud services.

03

Analytics and Tracking

Some applications use third-party analytics and attribution tools to understand app installations and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

Where an application uses the Facebook SDK provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. for app-install tracking, the SDK may transmit app events and technical information to Meta, such as app launch or activation events and device or network information, depending on the SDK configuration and your device settings.

This information is used to attribute app installations to advertising campaigns and measure advertising performance. The Facebook SDK is used for app-install tracking and advertising measurement, not to collect the content you create within our applications.

Meta processes information in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

04

Advertising

Some applications may be promoted through third-party advertising platforms, including Meta advertising services.

Where the Facebook SDK is integrated into an application, it may send app-install and activation events to Meta to measure advertising campaign performance and attribute installations to advertisements.

Depending on how the SDK is configured and the permissions granted by the user, this processing may involve data used for advertising measurement or tracking across apps and services. Where Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework requires permission, the application will request that permission before carrying out tracking that requires it.

Our applications do not display third-party advertisements unless explicitly stated otherwise for the relevant application.

05

Third-Party Services

Applications may use third-party services to provide specific features or functionality.

Where an application uses the Facebook SDK for app-install tracking, information may be transmitted to Meta Platforms, Inc. for advertising attribution and measurement. Meta's processing of this information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Other third-party services, where used, process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Meta's privacy policy is available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by our applications. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children unless explicitly stated otherwise for a particular application.

08

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. On supported Apple devices, you can also manage tracking permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Restricting tracking may limit the ability to attribute an installation to a particular advertising campaign, but it does not necessarily prevent all data from being collected or shared with third-party services.
